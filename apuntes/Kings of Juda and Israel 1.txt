KINGS OF JUDAH AND ISRAEL (CHRONOLOGY AND PROPHETS)

Part 2: Fall of the Northern Kingdom and Fall of Jerusalem

Kings of the Southern Kingdom of Judah (Continued):
- Uzziah (Azariah): Reigned 52 years until 777 B.C.E.
- Jotham: Faithful king, reigned 16 years (777 - 762 B.C.E.).
- Ahaz: Wicked king, reigned 16 years (762 - 746 B.C.E.).
- Hezekiah: Great religious reformer, reigned 29 years (746 - 716 B.C.E.). Witnessed deliverance from Sennacherib.
- Manasseh: Reigned 55 years (716 - 661 B.C.E.), longest reign of any king in Jerusalem. Later repented.
- Amon: Reigned 2 years (661 - 659 B.C.E.).
- Josiah: Righteous young king and restorer of true worship, reigned 31 years (659 - 628 B.C.E.).
- Jehoahaz: Reigned only 3 months (628 B.C.E.).
- Jehoiakim: Reigned 11 years (628 - 618 B.C.E.).
- Jehoiachin: Reigned 3 months and 10 days (618 - 617 B.C.E.). Taken captive to Babylon.
- Zedekiah: Last king on David's throne in Jerusalem, reigned 11 years (617 - 607 B.C.E.).
- 607 B.C.E.: Jerusalem and its temple are destroyed by the Babylonians under King Nebuchadnezzar.

Kings of the Northern Kingdom of Israel (Continued):
- Zechariah: Recorded reign of only 6 months (c. 803 B.C.E.). Assassinated by Shallum.
- Shallum: Reigned only 1 month (c. 791 B.C.E.).
- Menahem: Reigned 10 years (c. 791 - c. 780 B.C.E.). Paid tribute to Pul king of Assyria.
- Pekahiah: Reigned 2 years (c. 780 - c. 778 B.C.E.).
- Pekah: Reigned 20 years (c. 778 - c. 758 B.C.E.).
- Hoshea: Reigned 9 years from c. 748 B.C.E. Last king of Israel.
- 740 B.C.E.: Assyria conquers Samaria and subjugates Israel; the northern ten-tribe kingdom of Israel comes to its complete end.

Contemporary Prophets:
- Hosea: Prophesied in the northern kingdom (c. 800 - 745 B.C.E.).
- Micah: Prophesied in Judah during Jotham, Ahaz, and Hezekiah (c. 777 - 716 B.C.E.).
- Isaiah: Major prophet in Jerusalem (c. 778 - after 732 B.C.E.).
- Nahum: Prophesied against Nineveh (before 632 B.C.E.).
- Zephaniah: Active during early days of King Josiah (c. 650 B.C.E.).
- Jeremiah: Weeping prophet, witnessed the fall of Jerusalem (647 - 580 B.C.E.).
- Habakkuk: Active shortly before the Babylonian invasion (c. 628 B.C.E.).
- Obadiah: Prophesied against Edom (c. 607 B.C.E.).
- Ezekiel: Prophesied to the Jewish exiles in Babylon (c. 613 - 591 B.C.E.).
- Daniel: Statesman and prophet in Babylon (c. 618 - 536 B.C.E.).
