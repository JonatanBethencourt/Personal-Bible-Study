KINGS OF JUDAH AND ISRAEL (CHRONOLOGY AND PROPHETS)

Part 1: The Divided Kingdom to the 8th Century B.C.E.

Kings of Southern Two-Tribe Kingdom of Judah:
- Rehoboam: Reigned 17 years (997 - 980 B.C.E.).
- Abijah (Abijam): Reigned 3 years (980 - 978 B.C.E.).
- Asa: Reigned 41 years (978 - 937 B.C.E.).
- Jehoshaphat: Reigned 25 years (937 - 913 B.C.E.).
- Jehoram (Judah): Reigned 8 years (913 - c. 906 B.C.E.).
- Ahaziah (Judah): Reigned 1 year (c. 906 - c. 905 B.C.E.).
- Queen Athaliah: Usurped throne and ruled for 6 years (c. 905 - 898 B.C.E.).
- Jehoash: Reigned 40 years (898 - 858 B.C.E.).
- Amaziah: Reigned 29 years (858 - 829 B.C.E.).
- Uzziah (Azariah): Reigned 52 years (829 - 777 B.C.E.).

Kings of Northern Ten-Tribe Kingdom of Israel:
- Jeroboam: First king of the northern kingdom, reigned 22 years (997 - c. 976 B.C.E.).
- Nadab: Reigned 2 years (c. 976 - c. 975 B.C.E.).
- Baasha: Reigned 24 years (c. 975 - c. 952 B.C.E.).
- Elah: Reigned 2 years (c. 952 - c. 951 B.C.E.).
- Zimri: Reigned only 7 days (c. 951 B.C.E.).
- Omri and Tibni: Ruled divided for 4 years (c. 947 B.C.E.).
- Omri (alone): Reigned 8 years (c. 940 B.C.E.).
- Ahab: Reigned 22 years (c. 940 - c. 920 B.C.E.). Husband of Jezebel.
- Ahaziah (Israel): Reigned 2 years (c. 920 - c. 917 B.C.E.).
- Jehoram (Israel): Reigned 12 years (c. 917 - c. 905 B.C.E.).
- Jehu: Executed Ahab's house, reigned 28 years (c. 905 - 876 B.C.E.).
- Jehoahaz: Reigned 14 years (876 - c. 862 B.C.E.).
- Jehoahaz and Jehoash: Coreigned 3 years (c. 862 - c. 859 B.C.E.).
- Jehoash (alone): Reigned 16 years (c. 859 - c. 844 B.C.E.).
- Jeroboam II: Reigned 41 years (c. 844 - c. 803 B.C.E.).

Contemporary Prophets:
- Elijah: Active during the reigns of Ahab and Ahaziah in Israel (c. 940 - c. 905 B.C.E.).
- Elisha: Successor to Elijah, active during Jehoram, Jehu, Jehoahaz, and Jehoash (c. 917 - c. 859 B.C.E.).
- Jonah: Active around 850 B.C.E. during the era of Jeroboam II.
- Joel: Active in Judah around 820 B.C.E.
- Amos: Active in Israel around 810 B.C.E. during the reign of Jeroboam II and Uzziah.
